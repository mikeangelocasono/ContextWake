# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, specific, patient, and constructive. Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and focus technical disagreement on evidence and user safety. Do not publish another person’s private information or credentials.

Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, threats, unwanted sexual attention, trolling, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
